About us

Your local homekill, just up the road

Northland Homekill is a family-run homekill butchery at 5 Te Kahu Street, Haruru Falls, just out of Paihia. We process local animals for local families, and our chillers fill up with Northland's finest meat every season.

Professional, honest and reliable service at affordable prices. That's the whole idea.

  • Local and family run
  • Sausages made on site
  • Fair, affordable prices

FAQ

Before you book

Anything else? Call or text Brendon on 021 0277 8595.

How do I book my animal in?

Call or text 021 0277 8595, email us, or message us on Facebook. Bookings fill quickly, especially before Christmas, so get in early.

Where are you?

5 Te Kahu Street, Haruru Falls, just out of Paihia in the Bay of Islands. Ask when you book about where you're coming from.

Can you make sausages from my meat?

Yes. We hang, prep and pack fresh sausages on site. Talk to us about flavours and quantities when you book.

How much does it cost?

Prices depend on the animal and how you want it cut and packed. Give us a call and we'll talk you through it.

When can I pick up my meat?

We'll let you know when your lot is cut, packed and ready to collect from the chillers.
